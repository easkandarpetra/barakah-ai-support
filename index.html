Frequently asked questions

How do I restore a purchase?

Your subscription is tied to your Barakah AI account, not your device — just sign in with the same account on your new device or after reinstalling, and your Barakah Pro or Barakah Max subscription is restored automatically. There's nothing to tap.

How do I cancel or manage my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through the app store you subscribed with, not in the app.
On iPhone/iPad: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Barakah AI.
On Android: open the Google Play Store app → tap your profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Barakah AI → Cancel subscription.

Where do the prayer times come from?

Prayer times are sourced from JAKIM (Jabatan Kemajuan Islam Malaysia) for accuracy across Malaysia.

The AI gave an answer I'm not sure about — what should I do?

Barakah AI is a study aid based on the Quran and authentic Hadith, not a substitute for a qualified scholar. For personal religious rulings (fiqh), please consult a qualified scholar or imam.
