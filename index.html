Frequently asked questions

How do I restore a purchase?

Open the app → Premium → tap "Restore Purchases". This restores your Barakah Pro or Barakah Max subscription on a new device using the same Apple ID you subscribed with.

How do I cancel or manage my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through Apple, not in the app. On your iPhone/iPad: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Barakah AI.

Where do the prayer times come from?

Prayer times are sourced from JAKIM (Jabatan Kemajuan Islam Malaysia) for accuracy across Malaysia.

The AI gave an answer I'm not sure about — what should I do?

Barakah AI is a study aid based on the Quran and authentic Hadith, not a substitute for a qualified scholar. For personal religious rulings (fiqh), please consult a qualified scholar or imam.
