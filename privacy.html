Privacy Policy · Last updated 21 August 2026

This policy explains what information Barakah AI ("the app", "we", "us") collects, why, and how it's used. Barakah AI is developed by Easkandar Petra (Elite Application Studio).

Information we collect

DataWhy we collect it
Email address, display nameAccount creation and sign-in, via Firebase Authentication (email/password or Google Sign-In)
Location (while using the app)Calculating accurate prayer times for your area. Only used while the app is open; you can decline and prayer times will fall back to a manually selected location
Questions you ask the AI chatSent to our backend and processed by Anthropic's AI models to generate a response
Prayer tracking & Dzikir countsSaved to your account so your streaks and history sync across devices
Subscription statusManaged via Apple's In-App Purchase system and RevenueCat, linked to your account so premium features unlock correctly

Third parties we use

  • Firebase (Google) — authentication and data storage
  • Anthropic — processes your AI chat questions to generate answers
  • RevenueCat — subscription/purchase management
  • Apple — processes all in-app purchases and subscriptions
  • SendGrid (Twilio) — delivers account emails (e.g. password reset codes)

We do not sell your data, and we do not use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

Your choices

You can decline location access at any time in iOS Settings — prayer times will still work using a manually selected city.

To request access to, correction of, or deletion of your account data, email eliteapplicationstudio@gmail.com and we'll action it within a reasonable time.

Data retention

We keep your account data for as long as your account is active. If you request deletion, we delete your account data from our systems, except where we're required to retain records (e.g. for financial/tax purposes relating to subscription payments).

Children's privacy

Barakah AI is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: eliteapplicationstudio@gmail.com
